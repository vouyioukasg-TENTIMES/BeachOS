# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a professional, welcoming, and respectful project environment. Everyone should be able to contribute without harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or personal attack.

Expected behaviour includes:

- communicating clearly and respectfully;
- giving useful feedback about the work rather than the person;
- listening to people with direct operational experience;
- acknowledging mistakes and correcting them openly;
- protecting private business, staff, and customer information;
- considering accessibility, language, culture, and different levels of technical experience;
- prioritising safe outcomes over blame during incidents.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discriminatory language, threats, unwanted personal attention, deliberate disclosure of private information, retaliation, or sustained disruption of project work.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository discussions, reviews, project communication, meetings, venue testing, and other spaces where a person represents the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project owner or another designated maintainer. Repository-specific private contact details must be configured before outside contributors are invited.

Maintainers will review reports promptly, protect confidentiality as far as practical, and take proportionate action. Actions may include a private warning, removal of content, temporary restriction, or permanent removal from project spaces.

Anyone handling a report must disclose conflicts of interest and step aside when impartial review is not possible. Retaliation against a person who raises a good-faith concern is prohibited.

## Attribution

This project-specific policy is informed by widely used open-source community conduct standards. It is intentionally concise for the project’s current private team and should be reviewed before public community participation begins.
