# BeachOS Operational Digital Twin

## Purpose

The BeachOS Operational Digital Twin is the business’s trustworthy operational
mirror.

It connects the real hospitality operation with a current, explainable view of:

- what exists;
- what has been confirmed;
- who is responsible;
- what work is active or outstanding;
- what requires attention;
- what remains uncertain;
- which human decision comes next;
- what history the business may responsibly learn from.

The twin does not attempt to reproduce every physical detail. It mirrors only
facts that have an approved operational purpose and a responsible source. It
does not treat silence, elapsed time, historical averages, physical proximity,
or expected behavior as proof.

The twin is therefore not a perfect copy of reality. It is an honest model of
confirmed operational truth, with uncertainty visible wherever reality has not
yet been confirmed.

## The Relationship Between Reality and BeachOS

Reality always comes first.

BeachOS becomes trustworthy when responsible people confirm meaningful facts as
operations happen. The digital view can then support awareness, continuity,
coordination, advice, accountability, and learning.

When reality and BeachOS may differ, the difference must not be hidden. The
correct response is to:

1. preserve the last confirmed fact;
2. state what is now uncertain;
3. identify why the uncertainty matters;
4. find the responsible human who can establish the current truth;
5. confirm the outcome without rewriting history.

The twin does not become authoritative by being digital. It becomes dependable
because confirmed business facts, approved rules, and responsible human actions
remain aligned.

## The Twin Is Responsibility Driven

BeachOS mirrors responsibility as well as state.

A Venue being `OPEN`, a Customer Group being `ACTIVE`, or a Ticket being
`ACKNOWLEDGED` matters because each fact changes what responsible people may or
must decide next. A state cannot accept a handoff, authorize an action, observe
a guest, or exercise hospitality.

At every point, the twin should be able to distinguish:

- the operational scope;
- the current confirmed facts;
- the responsible human or department;
- the action that person is authorized to take;
- the result already confirmed;
- the uncertainty still requiring human judgment.

# Operational Entities

## Venue

### What exists in reality

A Venue is one physical hospitality operation with its own people, service
locations, departments, opening context, guest activity, financial activity,
and operating rhythm.

### What exists inside BeachOS

BeachOS recognizes one enduring Venue identity, its Organization, current
Business Date, configured operational departments, Service Locations, opening
status, and connected operational history.

### What responsibility exists

The Host coordinates opening and the live arrival context. Department owners,
Responsible Service Staff, preparation departments, Cashier, and management
retain their own defined responsibilities. No one Venue state replaces those
separate owners.

### What confirmed facts synchronize reality and BeachOS

- the Venue’s stable identity and approved configuration;
- the current Business Date;
- department readiness declarations;
- official opening for the Business Date;
- current Customer Groups, Service Locations, Orders, Tickets, Bills, Payments,
  and responsibility facts where approved;
- permanent operational reports and history.

### What uncertainty remains

BeachOS may not know unreported physical disruption, informal changes, guest
activity not yet acknowledged, or conditions whose business meaning has not
been approved. Venue Closing is not yet an approved complete lifecycle.

### What decisions remain human

Whether to open when eligible, how to respond to operational pressure, how to
coordinate departments and guests, when to escalate, and every unapproved
exception remain human decisions.

## Departments

### What exists in reality

Departments are teams with distinct operational purposes, such as Kitchen, Bar,
Beach Operations, Cleaning, Maintenance, Cashier, and Reception where relevant.

### What exists inside BeachOS

BeachOS recognizes the departments required by a Venue, their mandatory or
relevant opening context, their responsible people, current readiness
declarations, and confirmed department-specific work.

### What responsibility exists

Each department’s responsible person owns the truth of that department’s
opening readiness. Kitchen and Bar separately own their destination-specific
Tickets after acknowledgment. The Host coordinates but does not replace their
specialist responsibility.

### What confirmed facts synchronize reality and BeachOS

- configured department identity and opening requirement;
- named responsible person;
- `READY`, `NOT READY`, or `BLOCKED` declaration and required reason;
- later declarations as preparation changes;
- concrete Kitchen and Bar Tickets and acknowledgments.

### What uncertainty remains

Unreported problems, informal workarounds, internal assignment within a
department, preparation readiness, completion, and delivery remain unknown
unless separately approved and confirmed.

### What decisions remain human

How to prepare the department, whether it is genuinely ready, how to resolve a
problem, how to organize internal work, and when to escalate remain human.

## Staff

### What exists in reality

Staff are real people carrying different responsibilities during changing
operational situations.

### What exists inside BeachOS

BeachOS recognizes a Staff Member’s business identity, active status, Roles,
Capabilities, current approved responsibilities, attributable actions, and
relevant history.

### What responsibility exists

Roles describe business responsibility. Capabilities authorize protected
actions. Current responsibility identifies who owns a specific operational
scope now. These three facts are related but never interchangeable.

### What confirmed facts synchronize reality and BeachOS

- the person’s approved identity and current status;
- Role and Capability assignments;
- accepted responsibilities such as Responsible Waiter or department owner;
- consequential actions and approved handoffs;
- Shift context where confirmed and relevant.

### What uncertainty remains

Presence, attention, workload, informal assistance, physical custody, and
temporary delegation cannot be inferred merely from a Role or prior behavior.
The general delegation lifecycle remains unresolved.

### What decisions remain human

Whether to accept responsibility, how to perform hospitality work, when to ask
for help, how to coordinate, and how to exercise judgment within approved rules
remain human.

## Host

### What exists in reality

The Host is the person coordinating the Venue’s readiness, welcoming arriving
parties, understanding needs, guiding seating, and protecting continuity during
arrival and visible operational disruption.

### What exists inside BeachOS

BeachOS recognizes the Host’s current Venue context, opening responsibility,
confirmed department readiness, arrival context, available Service Locations,
known attention, and completed opening history.

### What responsibility exists

The Host owns coordination, not every department’s verification and not the
official opening of a Customer Group. Arrival responsibility remains with the
Host until an eligible Responsible Waiter accepts a clear handoff.

### What confirmed facts synchronize reality and BeachOS

- current Venue and Business Date;
- department declarations;
- official Venue opening;
- current Service Location facts;
- confirmed Responsible Waiter acceptance and Customer Group opening;
- known issues already represented by approved operational facts.

### What uncertainty remains

BeachOS cannot automatically know that a guest entered, was greeted, understood
an explanation, accepted a proposed location, or felt well served. Formal Host
handover, waiting, VIP, capacity, and accessibility rules remain incomplete.

### What decisions remain human

How to welcome, listen, communicate, offer choices, coordinate delays, protect
guest dignity, and decide when to involve another responsible person remain
human.

## Responsible Waiter

### What exists in reality

The Responsible Waiter is the one eligible person accountable for the Customer
Group’s active service relationship at that moment.

### What exists inside BeachOS

BeachOS recognizes the Responsible Waiter attached to the Customer Group,
Service Location, Orders, optional Bill, Payments through that Bill, and
preserved service history.

### What responsibility exists

The Responsible Waiter intentionally accepts the Host handoff, opens the
Customer Group, owns ongoing service coordination, and provides the preserved
responsible service context for Orders and downstream work.

### What confirmed facts synchronize reality and BeachOS

- eligibility and required Capability;
- explicit acceptance;
- `Responsible Waiter Assigned`;
- `Customer Group Opened`;
- accepted Orders and their service context;
- an approved later responsibility change, once its workflow is defined.

### What uncertainty remains

Informal cover, breaks, temporary assistance, handover acceptance, workload,
and delivery ownership are not fully defined. BeachOS cannot infer a new
Responsible Waiter from Zone assignment or physical presence.

### What decisions remain human

Whether to accept the group, how to serve it, how to coordinate preparation,
when to communicate, and when to request an approved handover remain human.

## Customer Group

### What exists in reality

A Customer Group is the party being served together as one continuing
operational relationship.

### What exists inside BeachOS

BeachOS recognizes one primary operational identity containing its current
Service Location, Responsible Waiter, multiple Orders, optional Bill, Payments,
and complete history.

### What responsibility exists

Exactly one Responsible Waiter owns service responsibility at a time. Other
departments own narrower work created for the group. The group itself owns its
continuing operational and financial context; it is not replaced when moved.

### What confirmed facts synchronize reality and BeachOS

- intentional Customer Group opening;
- assigned Responsible Waiter;
- current Service Location;
- every accepted Order Round;
- approved Financial Obligations, Bill Lines, and Payments;
- transfers and responsibility changes where approved;
- eventual completion when its conditions are approved.

### What uncertainty remains

BeachOS does not require individual guest identity initially. It may not know
party composition, informal departures, changing preferences, satisfaction, or
whether physical service has ended. Completion conditions remain unresolved.

### What decisions remain human

How to serve the party, whether another Order Round is appropriate, how to
respond to changing needs, and how to complete service under future approved
rules remain human.

## Service Location

### What exists in reality

A Service Location is the physical unit at which a Customer Group is served,
such as a Beach Sunbed set, Pool Sunbed set, or Upper Table.

### What exists inside BeachOS

BeachOS recognizes one stable Service Location identity, visible name or
number, type, Area context, dynamic Zone assignment, operational status,
Digital Identity, current Customer Group, and preserved history.

### What responsibility exists

The Host may propose and identify the location. The Responsible Waiter
intentionally opens the Customer Group there. Authorized staff may later
transfer the whole group. Identification never grants responsibility or
authority.

### What confirmed facts synchronize reality and BeachOS

- stable identity and visible marker;
- Digital Identity resolution or manual lookup;
- confirmed match between physical and digital context;
- availability or occupancy;
- Customer Group opening and transfer facts;
- relevant approved row charge at Beach Sunbed opening.

### What uncertainty remains

Physical damage, unreported occupancy, moved markers, cleanliness, and mismatch
between visible reality and the last confirmed state may remain uncertain.
Operational release after departure is unresolved.

### What decisions remain human

Whether the location suits the guests, whether physical conditions are safe,
whether to offer it, and how to resolve a mismatch remain human.

## Orders

### What exists in reality

Guests request items during one or more service rounds. Responsible service
staff review the request and submit it for preparation, supply, and financial
accountability.

### What exists inside BeachOS

BeachOS recognizes each submitted Order as one permanent Order Round with exact
items, quantities, notes, preserved prices, destinations, submission context,
and resulting Kitchen or Bar Tickets and Financial Obligations.

### What responsibility exists

Authorized service staff own review and intentional submission. Kitchen and Bar
receive only their destination-specific work. The Responsible Waiter remains
the Customer Group’s broader service owner.

### What confirmed facts synchronize reality and BeachOS

- active Customer Group and responsible service context;
- reviewed items and preserved commercial facts;
- `Order Submitted` or confirmed rejection;
- `Kitchen Ticket Created` and `Bar Ticket Created` where applicable;
- exact resulting Financial Obligations and Bill Lines.

### What uncertainty remains

An attempted submission may have an unknown outcome and must be reconciled.
BeachOS cannot infer preparation, delivery, consumption, satisfaction,
cancellation, or correction without approved facts.

### What decisions remain human

What the guests requested, whether the draft is correct, when to submit, how to
clarify notes, and how to respond to later changes remain human.

## Kitchen

### What exists in reality

Kitchen Staff receive confirmed food work, organize preparation, handle
physical ingredients and dishes, and coordinate with service staff.

### What exists inside BeachOS

BeachOS recognizes concrete Kitchen Tickets containing only Kitchen-destination
items, the originating Order and Customer Group, submission and current Service
Location context, Responsible Service Staff, and acknowledgement history.

### What responsibility exists

Kitchen owns its confirmed Ticket work after intentional acknowledgment.
Kitchen responsibility remains distinct from Bar responsibility and the
Responsible Waiter’s wider Customer Group responsibility.

### What confirmed facts synchronize reality and BeachOS

- `Kitchen Ticket Created` from an accepted Order;
- Kitchen’s intentional `Kitchen Ticket Acknowledged`;
- original and current service context;
- future Ready or Completed facts only after their meanings are approved.

### What uncertainty remains

Physical preparation progress, priority, station ownership, delay, partial
readiness, completion, collection, Runner custody, and delivery are not
currently confirmed business facts.

### What decisions remain human

How to organize and perform preparation, how to respond to real conditions, and
how to coordinate unresolved handoffs remain human. BeachOS must not declare
readiness from elapsed time.

## Bar

### What exists in reality

Bar Staff receive confirmed drink and supply work, prepare or provide items,
hold them where necessary, and coordinate their handoff.

### What exists inside BeachOS

BeachOS recognizes concrete Bar Tickets containing only Bar-destination items,
the originating context, and confirmed Bar acknowledgment.

### What responsibility exists

Bar owns its acknowledged Ticket work. That ownership is independent from
Kitchen even when both Tickets belong to the same Order Round.

### What confirmed facts synchronize reality and BeachOS

- `Bar Ticket Created`;
- `Bar Ticket Acknowledged`;
- preserved submission and current Service Location context;
- future Ready, Completed, or delivery facts only after approval.

### What uncertainty remains

Prepared versus packaged items, direct handoff, physical custody, readiness,
completion, Runner involvement, and guest delivery remain unresolved.

### What decisions remain human

How to prepare or supply the work, how to coordinate direct or indirect
handoff, and how to manage real operational conditions remain human.

## Bills

### What exists in reality

A Customer Group incurs exact financial obligations for approved reasons and
owes a running amount until valid Payments and approved closing actions resolve
it.

### What exists inside BeachOS

BeachOS recognizes at most one Bill for a Customer Group, created only by the
first Financial Obligation, with permanent Bill Lines, Payments, total, Balance,
status, and history.

### What responsibility exists

The business owns financial truth. Authorized staff own specific actions such
as recording Payments, corrections, and closing under approved rules. The
Responsible Waiter owns service context but does not automatically own every
financial authority.

### What confirmed facts synchronize reality and BeachOS

- each approved `Financial Obligation Created`;
- `Bill Created` only at the first obligation;
- each exact `Bill Line Added`;
- valid Payments and resulting Balance;
- `Bill Fully Paid` at zero Balance;
- future authorized closing or correction facts.

### What uncertainty remains

Unapproved waivers, corrections, split or partial settlement, cash differences,
fiscal outcomes, and closing exceptions cannot be represented as settled truth.
Zero Balance does not prove operational completion.

### What decisions remain human

How to explain the Bill, resolve a dispute, approve an exception, and decide
whether all closing conditions are satisfied remain human and capability
governed.

## Payments

### What exists in reality

Money is offered, processed, received, refused, reversed, or otherwise handled
through a real Payment Method.

### What exists inside BeachOS

BeachOS recognizes a Payment only when the business has confirmed receipt of an
exact amount against one Bill through an approved Payment Method.

### What responsibility exists

An authorized Staff Member is accountable for confirming and recording the
Payment exactly once. Sensitive actions require traceability without turning
the history into surveillance.

### What confirmed facts synchronize reality and BeachOS

- Bill identity and current accepted state;
- exact amount and approved Payment Method;
- responsible actor and time;
- `Payment Recorded` only after receipt succeeds;
- updated Balance and `Bill Fully Paid` where applicable.

### What uncertainty remains

An interrupted payment may have an unknown outcome. Partial, split, excess,
reversed, refunded, cash-change, tip, and post-closing behavior remain
unresolved.

### What decisions remain human

Whether receipt genuinely succeeded, how to recover an uncertain result, how to
handle the guest conversation, and whether a permitted exception applies remain
human.

## Guests

### What exists in reality

Guests are people arriving with needs, preferences, expectations, changing
decisions, and experiences that may never be fully observable.

### What exists inside BeachOS

Before official service, BeachOS may know only an arriving party’s current
operational context. After opening, the Customer Group is the temporary
operational identity. Individual customer identities are not required in the
initial model.

### What responsibility exists

The Host owns arrival coordination. The Responsible Waiter owns ongoing service
responsibility after acceptance. Guests remain the human participants in
hospitality, not objects controlled by the twin.

### What confirmed facts synchronize reality and BeachOS

- human acknowledgment of arrival;
- expressed needs that are necessary and responsibly confirmed;
- accepted Service Location and Responsible Waiter handoff;
- Customer Group opening;
- approved Orders, Payments, and later departure facts where defined.

### What uncertainty remains

BeachOS cannot infer identity, preference, accessibility needs, VIP status,
satisfaction, intent, consumption, or departure from appearance or assumption.
Waiting and guest-profile lifecycles are not approved.

### What decisions remain human

What guests want, whether they accept an option, how they experience service,
when they wish to order or leave, and what compassionate response they need
remain human realities interpreted through conversation.

## Business Date

### What exists in reality

A hospitality operating day has a business meaning that may cross midnight and
must remain coherent for opening, live work, financial facts, and history.

### What exists inside BeachOS

BeachOS recognizes the Venue’s current Business Date according to its approved
local policy. It provides the shared scope for daily opening and operational
history.

### What responsibility exists

The business approves the policy. The Host opens the Venue for the confirmed
Business Date. Every responsible person must act in the correct current scope.

### What confirmed facts synchronize reality and BeachOS

- Venue-local policy and time context;
- current Business Date;
- one official opening and Opening Report for that date;
- operations attributable to the correct day.

### What uncertainty remains

If policy, current date, or the outcome of an opening cannot be confirmed,
BeachOS must not assume yesterday, calendar midnight, or a convenient date.
Venue Closing for the date is unresolved.

### What decisions remain human

The operating policy, exceptional-day treatment, and later closing decision
remain business and human responsibilities.

## Operational Timeline

### What exists in reality

The operating day unfolds as a connected sequence: preparation, opening,
arrival, seating, service, ordering, preparation, financial settlement,
departure, reset, and closing. Several activities may overlap.

### What exists inside BeachOS

BeachOS recognizes an ordered history of confirmed business facts and the
current operational context produced by those facts. Illustrative times do not
become standards merely because they appear in a journey.

### What responsibility exists

Each fact belongs to a responsible human or approved business outcome. The
timeline supports continuity among different owners without collapsing their
responsibilities.

### What confirmed facts synchronize reality and BeachOS

- attributable Domain Events;
- approved Activity Logs;
- permanent Orders, Tickets, Bills, Payments, reports, and responsibility
  changes;
- explicit rejections and known uncertain outcomes where approved.

### What uncertainty remains

Physical actions between confirmed facts may remain invisible. Missing facts do
not prove inactivity, and elapsed time does not prove delay, readiness, or
completion without an approved rule.

### What decisions remain human

How to sequence work under real pressure, when to interrupt or escalate, and
how to adapt hospitality without violating approved rules remain human.

## Responsibility Timeline

### What exists in reality

Responsibility moves and overlaps as the day proceeds: department owners prepare
the Venue, the Host coordinates arrival, the Responsible Waiter accepts the
Customer Group, and Kitchen and Bar accept concrete preparation work.

### What exists inside BeachOS

BeachOS recognizes the current owner of each approved scope and preserves
confirmed assignments, acknowledgments, transfers, and ending facts as a
chronological responsibility history.

### What responsibility exists

At any moment, responsibility should have a clear scope and owner. Concurrent
responsibilities are coordinated rather than treated as vague shared ownership.

### What confirmed facts synchronize reality and BeachOS

- configured department owner;
- Host opening and arrival context;
- Responsible Waiter acceptance and assignment;
- Kitchen and Bar Ticket acknowledgments;
- future accepted handoffs and ending conditions once approved.

### What uncertainty remains

Waiter handover, temporary delegation, Runner ownership, custody, fulfilment
handoff, payment ownership, departure, and closing responsibility remain
incomplete.

### What decisions remain human

Whether to accept a handoff, request support, transfer responsibility, or retain
ownership during uncertainty remains human under approved rules.

## Operational Memory

### What exists in reality

The business accumulates experience through completed days, recurring
situations, exceptions, improvements, and knowledge held by staff.

### What exists inside BeachOS

Operational Memory is the deliberately preserved collection of confirmed
facts, relevant context, approved standards, responsibility history, reviewed
outcomes, and approved improvement actions.

### What responsibility exists

The business owns the purpose, definitions, retention, review, and appropriate
use of memory. Responsible humans interpret it. BeachOS preserves and explains;
it does not turn history into authority.

### What confirmed facts synchronize reality and BeachOS

- permanent business history already required by the Domain;
- approved context such as Venue, Business Date, responsibility, and known
  exception;
- reviewed outcomes and approved operational changes;
- evidence that a later outcome actually occurred.

### What uncertainty remains

History may be incomplete, non-comparable, affected by context, or no longer
relevant. Unrecorded experience and personal knowledge may remain outside the
twin. Retention rules require approved purpose.

### What decisions remain human

What deserves preservation, which contexts are comparable, what should be
forgotten, and what lesson the business accepts remain human governance
decisions.

## Operational Learning

### What exists in reality

Hospitality teams observe incidents, repetition, seasonal conditions, changed
practice, and improvements over time.

### What exists inside BeachOS

Operational Learning is the explainable use of confirmed historical facts to
distinguish one-time incidents, recurring problems, seasonal patterns,
operational drift, consistency, and observed improvement.

### What responsibility exists

BeachOS may bring explainable patterns to the attention of the human responsible
for review. The business approves standards and changes. Historical repetition
never grants BeachOS authority to create a rule.

### What confirmed facts synchronize reality and BeachOS

- a clear approved objective or standard;
- sufficiently comparable confirmed situations;
- relevant context and known exceptions;
- the action or change reviewed by the business;
- later confirmed outcomes showing whether it helped.

### What uncertainty remains

A pattern may be coincidence, context dependent, or supported by incomplete
facts. BeachOS must not invent certainty, scores, hidden causes, predictions, or
judgments about people.

### What decisions remain human

Whether a pattern matters, why it occurred, whether practice should change,
whether coaching is appropriate, and when an improvement becomes an approved
standard remain human.

# Synchronization Principles

The Operational Digital Twin follows these principles:

1. **Reality precedes representation.** BeachOS never changes what happened by
   changing how it describes it.
2. **Only confirmed facts become operational truth.** Expectations, silence,
   elapsed time, and probability do not synchronize the twin.
3. **Every consequential fact has a responsible source.** The twin should know
   who or what approved business outcome established the fact.
4. **Responsibility and state remain connected but distinct.** A state informs
   action; it does not accept ownership.
5. **Current facts outrank history.** Yesterday’s truth and long-term patterns
   cannot override today’s confirmed operation.
6. **Uncertainty is part of the twin.** Unknown, conflicting, or potentially
   stale information must remain visible.
7. **Physical and digital identity must agree.** Identification locates context
   but never authorizes or performs an action.
8. **Operational and financial truth remain exact.** Orders, obligations,
   Bills, and Payments must never be inferred, silently rewritten, or lost.
9. **History preserves explanation.** Corrections and changes add truth; they do
   not erase the original fact.
10. **Humans retain hospitality judgment.** BeachOS supports the decision with
    facts, attention, and explainable advice.
11. **Learning advises; it never governs silently.** Repetition can justify
    review, not automatic policy.
12. **The twin mirrors only what has an approved purpose.** More captured detail
    is not automatically more operational truth.

# What Would BeachOS Know at Any Moment During a Real Operating Day?

At any moment, BeachOS should be able to say—within the limits of confirmed
facts:

- which Organization and Venue are in scope;
- which Business Date governs the operation;
- whether the Venue has officially opened for that date;
- which mandatory departments declared `READY`, `NOT READY`, or `BLOCKED`, who
  declared each fact, and why attention is required;
- who currently owns each approved operational responsibility;
- which arriving parties have become active Customer Groups and which human
  arrival facts remain outside the twin;
- which Service Locations exist, how they are identified, which are confirmed
  available or occupied, and where physical/digital agreement is uncertain;
- which Responsible Waiter owns each active Customer Group;
- which Order Rounds were safely accepted, what each contained, and which
  preparation and financial consequences were confirmed;
- which concrete Kitchen and Bar Tickets exist and which have been acknowledged
  by their departments;
- that Ticket readiness, completion, custody, and delivery remain unknown until
  their Product Decisions and confirming facts exist;
- which Financial Obligations and Bill Lines explain each running Bill;
- which Payments have been confirmed, what Balance remains, and whether zero
  Balance has been reached without pretending that closure or departure
  automatically followed;
- which consequential actions succeeded, were rejected, or have uncertain
  outcomes requiring reconciliation;
- what currently deserves immediate action, ordinary attention, or quiet
  monitoring under approved rules;
- which relevant facts changed over the operational timeline;
- how responsibility moved over the responsibility timeline where approved;
- what permanent history the business may rely upon;
- which historical patterns are explainable enough to deserve human review;
- what BeachOS does not know;
- which responsible human must still decide what happens next.

The Digital Twin is trustworthy not when it claims to know everything, but when
the owner can distinguish, at any moment, what the business has confirmed, what
remains uncertain, who is responsible, and whether the digital view still
matches the real hospitality operation.
